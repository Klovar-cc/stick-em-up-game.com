how to play?

the rules of engagement

stack 'em high! roll the die, make your tower reach the sky.

build your base, load the powder, store your ammo, make 'em cower.

Stick 'Em Up!™ when you roll, one well-aimed shot: they pay the toll.

rolls 1, 2 & 3 (the build): place your card flat, or fold it into a "taco" or "burger" for structural integrity.

rolls 4 & 5 (the bandolier): tear a piece off. place the sticky part on your tower. save the non-sticky extra in your bandolier. (note: you can only crumple scraps into ammo once you have a stack of exactly 6.)

roll 6 (the showdown): yell "Stick 'Em Up!". choose one completed 6-stack ammo ball from your bandolier and target an opponent's tower.

the golden rule: you may only touch the card you are placing and the one card it connects to. hands off the rest.
