the rules of engagement

  • the core loop: flip the sand timer. roll the d6. rip, fold, and place your sticky card.
  • 1. the flat: place it flat.
  • 2 & 3. the taco & burger: fold the card so sticky sides face in or out.
  • 4 & 5. the tear: tear a piece off. place the sticky part. save the non-sticky extra in your "holster".
  • 6. shoot 'em low: yell "stick 'em up!". target an opponent. crumple your holster scraps into a ball and throw it at their tower.
  • the golden rule: you may only touch the card you are placing and the one card it connects to. hands off the rest.
