🤠 Stick-Em Up!™
Stick-Em Up!™ is a fast-paced tabletop showdown where your only weapons are a stack of sticky cards and a d6.

🏗️ The Rules of the Range
In this territory, reaching the clouds is the only way to win.

Roll: Toss a standard d6 to see what the trail has in store for you.

Stick: Deploy your sticky cards to build your tower.

Build: Height is the law of the land, but a shaky foundation will bring you down.

The High Noon Finish: To claim the title, you must have the tallest (or highest) tower still standing when the dust settles at the end of the game.
